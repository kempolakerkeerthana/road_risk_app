Predict accident severity

Severity means impact on traffic: 1 = short delay, 2 = moderate delay, 3 = long delay, 4 = very long delay (as defined in the US Accidents data).
The model was trained on US accident data only and has no Indian data. For an Indian state the location is ignored and the score is an average over US locations. Time, weather and road features still count.
